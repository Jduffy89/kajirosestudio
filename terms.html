Baby & Infant Safety

Kaji Rose Studio baby blankets are designed for comfort, warmth, and supervised use. They are not intended to replace safe-sleep practices and are not designed or marketed as infant sleep products.

  • Never leave an infant unattended with a blanket.
  • Do not place loose blankets, including Kaji Rose Studio blankets, in a baby's crib, bassinet, play yard, or other sleep space.
  • Do not use this blanket as a substitute for properly fitted infant sleep clothing designed for safe sleep.
  • Follow current safe-sleep recommendations from your pediatrician and recognized child-safety organizations.
  • Stop using the blanket if it becomes damaged, excessively worn, develops loose threads, or otherwise becomes unsuitable for use.

Adult supervision is required whenever an infant or young child is using or in contact with the blanket. Kaji Rose Studio does not claim its blankets are safe for unsupervised infant sleep and does not recommend their use for that purpose.
